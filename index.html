From inspiration to a decision

How to use an OrientDig spreadsheet

An OrientDig spreadsheet helps organize product discovery: names, source links, selected options, reference prices and research notes in one place. This page provides a browser-based selection rather than a downloadable Google Sheet. Its catalog comes from CAFANS, so confirm whether your intended agent can purchase the original listing.

01 / FIND THE EXACT ITEM

Keep the source and variant together.

Open CAFANS and locate the product by name. Save the original seller URL, item identifier, color, size and quantity. Similar titles can describe different versions; compare the source photographs and options before treating two listings as a match.

02 / CHECK THE DETAILS

Measure before choosing a size.

For a hoodie or T-shirt, compare chest width, shoulder width and length against clothing you own. For footwear, check the seller’s measurement method and size chart. A familiar size label alone does not establish fit.

03 / CONFIRM THE ORDER

Resolve unknowns before payment.

Confirm supported purchasing links, available stock, fees and return terms with your chosen agent. Review shipping restrictions and obtain an applicable delivery quote. Save the confirmation date with your shortlist so old information stays identifiable.

A little more clarity

OrientDig spreadsheet FAQ

Is this the official OrientDig website?

No. This is an independent product discovery guide. Its displayed product selection is sourced from CAFANS. The OrientDig name describes the shopping topic; it does not imply endorsement, affiliation or a verified purchasing integration.

Where do the two main buttons take me?

Both “OrientDig spreadsheet” and “Buy it now” open https://cafans.com. The second button opens the catalog destination; it does not create an order, select a variant or charge a payment.

Are these real products or example listings?

The 12 names and estimated USD prices were taken from CAFANS’ featured product directory on November 19, 2026. Two listing images were also retrieved. Availability, price changes, authenticity and purchase compatibility have not been independently verified.

Does the displayed price include shipping?

The imported amounts are reference item prices from the source homepage. Do not assume they include domestic shipping, international freight, service fees, payment charges or taxes. Confirm the currency and selected variant on the current listing.

Do product photos count as QC photos?

Not automatically. Seller or catalog images illustrate a listing; inspection photos should be identified by their origin and related order. No verified QC gallery is provided here. Ask your agent about inspections for your actual purchase, and keep reference photos separate from order-specific evidence.

Can I use every CAFANS listing with OrientDig?

This page does not establish that. Save the original seller URL and ask OrientDig whether that specific item is supported. Do not infer support from the title of this guide or the existence of a catalog entry.

Is the directory updated automatically?

No. This standalone page contains a dated snapshot. Its filters operate on those 12 saved entries. Open CAFANS for the current directory and check details again before making a decision.

Can I download this as a spreadsheet?

A downloadable spreadsheet is not included. You can create your own shortlist using the comparison fields above: source link, exact variant, price, currency, measurements, photo notes, charges and checked date.
